# QuteNote — every screen in the app today

For context when you are designing a new screen or redesigning an old one.
Titles in quotes are the real words on the screen.

## Getting in
- **Welcome** — "Send something real." with the wax seal being pressed live on the postmark.
- **Log in** — "Slide into their mailbox." Email or username, Apple and Google buttons, "New here? Create an account" underneath.
- **Create account** — name stacked over email, stamp-box fields, a birthday cake in the title band.
- **Pick your handle** — for people arriving through Apple or Google.
- **Your address** — "Where you at?" Needed before anything can be mailed.
- **Your birthday** — optional, skippable.

## The four tabs
- **Home** — search for a QT at the top, your status box, a carousel of cards (invite your people, add a photo, teach your handwriting, turn on alerts, the lavender dusk dates card), then the feed of friends' statuses with a heart button to send them something.
- **QTs** — your people, birthday countdowns, sync contacts, invite by text, search, and the invite box with the heart hands.
- **Dates** — birthdays and important dates, contact birthdays you can import, add a date with scroll wheels. Date types: Birthday, Pet-iversary, Tiny Triumphs, Sweet Traditions, Seasonal Feels.
- **Profile** — your wax seal stamp with your initial, sent and received cards as a grid you can open, your QR code, notifications and settings beside it, log out at the bottom.

## Sending a card (the SEND stamp in the middle)
"Send a little somethin'", four steps:
1. **Pick a card** — upload your own photo or choose from the artist library.
2. **Who's it for** — your QTs, or "Not on QuteNote yet?" invite them by text.
3. **Write it** — pick a pen, type the note, add a voice note, madlibs and prompts.
4. **Look it over** — the printed card preview with the stamp, send now or schedule it, save draft, MAIL IT.

## Everything else
- **Settings** — notifications, scheduled cards, drafts, secret language key, Deets (your account details), delete account.
- **Notifications** — five switches plus an Everything master switch.
- **Handwriting studio** — teach the app your handwriting by writing ten little words, then every letter becomes a font. Fix one letter by tapping it.
- **Secret language key** — her hand-drawn cipher alphabet and how the vowels tuck.
- **Scan a card** — a QR on the back of a printed card opens the card: reply, decode the secret message, listen to the voice note.
- **Invite page** — share your link, every 10th friend who joins earns you a free card.
- **Scheduled cards**, **Drafts**, **Extra card checkout**, **Admin** (launch list, analytics dashboard).
