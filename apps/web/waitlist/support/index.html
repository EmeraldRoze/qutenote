Support

Last updated October 29, 2026

Write to hello@qutenote.com and you will get a real answer, usually within a day or two. Tell us your username and, if something looks broken, send a screenshot.

Common things

I cannot log in

Use Forgot password on the login screen. Your email and username are not case sensitive, so capital letters are not the problem. If the reset email does not arrive, check spam, then write to us.

Where is my card?

Cards are printed and handed to the postal service, which usually takes a few days to deliver inside the United States. If it has been more than two weeks, write to us and we will reprint it or refund you.

Importing birthdays

On the Dates screen you can pull birthdays from your phone's contacts or from Google Contacts. We only read names and birthdays, we only keep the people you choose, and we never message anyone in your list.

Deleting your account

In the app: Settings, then Account, then Delete account. It erases your profile, addresses, dates, connections, and cards, and it cannot be undone. Prefer we do it? Email hello@qutenote.com from the address on your account.

Becoming an artist

Settings, then Become a QuteNote artist. You send samples and a photo ID, we review by hand, and you earn on every card sent with your art.
