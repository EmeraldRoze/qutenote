Accessibility

Last updated October 29, 2026

QuteNote is built by Mixtape Labs LLC, and we want it to work for everyone, including people who use a screen reader, navigate by keyboard, or need larger text and stronger contrast.

What we aim for

We measure ourselves against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines, WCAG 2.1, at level AA, on both the website and the app. We test every screen with automated tooling and fix what it finds.

What we have done

  • Every image carries alternative text, and decorative art is marked so screen readers skip it.
  • Every form field has a label, and every icon-only button has a spoken name.
  • Text meets the 4.5 to 1 contrast minimum against its background.
  • Keyboard focus is always visible, and a skip link jumps past the menu.
  • Animation respects your system setting for reduced motion.

Where we know we fall short

Text in the app does not yet grow when you raise the text size in your phone's settings. We are working on it. If that makes QuteNote hard for you to use, tell us and we will help you directly in the meantime.

Tell us

If anything here is hard to use, write to hello@qutenote.com and say what happened and what device you were on. We answer, and we treat it as a bug, not a suggestion.
