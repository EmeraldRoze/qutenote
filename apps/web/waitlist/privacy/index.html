Privacy policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

QuteNote is run by Mixtape Labs LLC. This explains what we collect, why, and what we will never do with it. If anything here is unclear, write to hello@qutenote.com and a person will answer.

What we collect

  • Your account. Your name, username, email, and if you choose, your photo and birthday.
  • Your mailing address. So cards can reach you.
  • What you write and send. The words on your cards, the art you pick, photos you upload, voice notes you record, and the handwriting samples you give us so we can print in your hand.
  • Your dates and your QTs. Birthdays and occasions you save, and the people you connect with.
  • Basic usage. Which screens get opened and which buttons get tapped, so we can tell what is confusing. No advertising trackers, ever.

Addresses

A mailing address is the most sensitive thing we hold, so it gets the strictest rule in this document. An address is used for one purpose: printing and mailing a card. It is encrypted where it is stored. It is never shown to the person sending the card. You can send someone a card without ever learning where they live, and they can receive one without handing you their address.

Contacts and Google

If you ask us to, QuteNote can read your phone's address book or your Google Contacts to find birthdays worth remembering. That only happens when you tap the button and grant permission, and here is exactly what we do with it:

  • We read names, email addresses, and birthdays.
  • We keep only the people you choose to add, and only their name and the month and day. We do not store the year.
  • We never store your Google password or keep long-term access to your Google account. The permission we receive lasts minutes and is used once.
  • We never message, email, or mail anyone from your contacts. Nothing is sent on your behalf, ever.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share contact data with anyone.

QuteNote's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is never used for advertising, never sold, and never used to train any model.

Who else sees your data

Only the companies that make the product work, and only the piece each one needs: our print and mail partner gets the card and the delivery address, our payment processor handles your payment (we never see your card number), our hosting and database providers store the app, and our email service sends things like password resets. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Deleting everything

In the app, open Settings, then Account, then Delete account. That erases your profile, your addresses, your dates, your connections, and the cards you have written. It cannot be undone. A card already in the mail cannot be recalled, and we keep the minimum billing records the law requires. You can also write to hello@qutenote.com and ask us to do it for you.

Children

QuteNote is for people 13 and over. If we learn we have data from a child under 13, we delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a way that matters, we will say so in the app rather than quietly editing this page. Questions go to hello@qutenote.com.
